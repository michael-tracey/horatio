# Horatio user manual

Horatio keeps the characters, experience, downtimes and game nights of Mind's Eye Theatre LARP troupes in one place. This manual explains how to use it, task by task, using the app's own labels.

Every part is open to everyone: players are welcome to read how staff run things, and the other way round.

## Contents

- [Getting started](getting-started.md): for everyone. Signing up and signing in, your account and settings, notifications, the calendar link, and where to find help.
- [Players](players.md): for players. Building a character, spending XP, your home troupe, your backstory, downtimes, and playing at a game.
- [Running a troupe](running-a-troupe.md): for troupe staff, from the lead storyteller to narrators. Setting up a troupe, house rules, approvals, downtime cycles, game nights and the troupe's records.
- [Custodian](custodian.md): for the custodian, who looks after the whole site. Troupe requests, people's accounts, backups and the system.
- [Glossary](glossary.md): for anyone. Horatio's words, such as home troupe, Play View and house rule, in plain English.

## Reading it in the app

In Horatio, choose **Help** in the menu at the top of any page. You can read it without signing in. On the Help page, search finds a word in every part at once.

## About this manual

The manual lives in the project's repository, in `docs/manual`, and the app shows the same files. If something here doesn't match what you see, or you'd like something explained, tell your storytellers or the custodian, or open an issue on [the project's GitHub page](https://github.com/michael-tracey/horatio).
