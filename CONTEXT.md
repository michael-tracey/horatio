# Horatio

Horatio keeps the record of characters in Mind's Eye Theatre LARP troupes, and
runs the downtimes those characters take between games.

## Language

### People and groups

**Player**:
A person who plays one or more characters.
_Avoid_: User (for the person in the game), member

**Troupe**:
The top-level group: it runs games, has its own staff and house rules, and characters belong to it. Nothing sits above a troupe.
_Avoid_: Game, chapter, chronicle, organisation

**Staff**:
The people who run a troupe: its storytellers, assistant storytellers and narrators.
_Avoid_: Admins, ST team

**Storyteller**:
A staff member who leads a troupe, and the only role that manages the troupe's staff and house rules.
_Avoid_: ST (in names), head ST, GM, admin

**Assistant Storyteller**:
A staff member who can do everything a storyteller can except manage staff and house rules.
_Avoid_: AST (in names), sub-ST

**Narrator**:
A staff member who can do whatever a player in the troupe can, and also answer the downtime actions assigned to them.
_Avoid_: Helper, volunteer

### Characters

**Character**:
A persona a player plays, belonging to exactly one splat.
_Avoid_: Sheet (that is the character's record, not the character), PC

**Splat**:
The kind of supernatural a character is, such as Vampire, Werewolf or Changeling.
_Avoid_: Venue, creature type, game line

**Trait**:
A single rated or named entry on a character's sheet, such as a skill, merit, flaw, discipline or influence.
_Avoid_: Stat, ability

### Rules

**Rule Set**:
The generic rules for a splat that the system ships with, including what each trait costs.
_Avoid_: Core rules, ruleset (one word)

**House Rule**:
A troupe's change to one value in a rule set, such as the cost of a merit or skill, or an allowance rule.
_Avoid_: Override, custom rule, homebrew

**Troupe Rules**:
A rule set with one troupe's house rules applied. A character plays by its home troupe's troupe rules, or by the plain rule set if it has no home troupe.
_Avoid_: Effective rules, local rules

### Character records

**Approved Sheet**:
The character's sheet as last approved by a storyteller or assistant storyteller; edits awaiting approval are not part of it.
_Avoid_: Current sheet, live sheet

**Purchase**:
A trait bought for a character, recorded with the cost paid at the time; later changes to the rules never alter it.
_Avoid_: Spend, buy

**Approval**:
A storyteller's or assistant storyteller's acceptance of a change a player has asked for.
_Avoid_: Sign-off, review

**Home Troupe**:
The one troupe whose downtime cycles a character takes part in, chosen from the troupes the character belongs to.
_Avoid_: Main troupe, primary troupe

**Home Troupe Change**:
A player's request to move a character's home troupe, decided by the storytellers or assistant storytellers of the new troupe and taking effect from that troupe's next cycle.
_Avoid_: Transfer

### Downtimes

**Downtime**:
What a character does between games, submitted by the player and answered by staff.
_Avoid_: DT (in names), between-game actions

**Downtime Cycle**:
One round of downtimes for a troupe, with an open date, a close date and a grace period.
_Avoid_: Month, period, sheet

**Downtime Form**:
The questions a downtime cycle asks, started as a copy of the troupe's previous form.
_Avoid_: Template, Google Form

**Question**:
One prompt on a downtime form; either an action question or a custom question.
_Avoid_: Field, item

**Action Question**:
A question that spends one of the character's actions of a given action kind.
_Avoid_: Action field

**Custom Question**:
A question storytellers or assistant storytellers add that spends no action, such as a nomination or plot prompt.
_Avoid_: Extra question, survey question

**Action Kind**:
A category of downtime action, such as general, feeding, investigation, or elite or underworld influence.
_Avoid_: Action type, category

**Action Allowance**:
How many actions of each action kind a character may take in one downtime cycle, fixed when the cycle opens; unused actions are lost.
_Avoid_: Action count, slots

**Allowance Rule**:
A rule saying how a trait adds to or removes from an action allowance.
_Avoid_: Bonus, modifier

**Allowance Adjustment**:
A storyteller's or assistant storyteller's change to one character's action allowance for one cycle, with a stated reason.
_Avoid_: Override

**Submission**:
A character's answers to one downtime cycle's form.
_Avoid_: Entry, form response

**Response**:
A staff member's answer to one question in a submission.
_Avoid_: Result, reply, outcome

**Assignment**:
The staff member responsible for writing a response, or the troupe's shared pool when no one has claimed it.
_Avoid_: Colour, task owner

**Delivery**:
Sending a submission's responses to its player.
_Avoid_: Send, publish
