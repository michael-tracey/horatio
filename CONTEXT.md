# Horatio

Horatio keeps the record of characters in Mind's Eye Theatre LARP troupes, and
runs the downtimes those characters take between games.

## Language

### People and groups

**Player**:
A person who plays one or more characters. A player sees only their own characters' sheets, never other players'.
_Avoid_: User (for the person in the game), member

**Profile**:
What anyone signed in can see about a player: their display name, handle, pronouns, when they joined, a bio, social media links and their Discord username. Never their email, which only they and the custodian see.
_Avoid_: Bio page, user page

**Handle**:
The unique name a person is found and shown by, such as @night_owl, in place of their email. They choose it, can sign in with it, and can change it once in 30 days; an old handle stays theirs for 90.
_Avoid_: Username, login

**Troupe**:
The top-level group: it runs games, has its own staff and house rules, and characters join it. No group sits above a troupe.
_Avoid_: Game, chapter, chronicle, organisation

**Troupe Profile**:
What a troupe tells players and people thinking of joining: its portrait, links, schedule and upcoming games, venue, setting, whether it takes new players, and age and content notes. Its storytellers edit it. Anyone signed in can see it, and anyone at all if the troupe makes it public. It never shows emails, characters or anything only members see, and the venue's street address only to people signed in.
_Avoid_: Troupe page, listing

**Archived Troupe**:
A troupe its lead storyteller has deleted: closed to all use, but kept with its full history. Troupes are never truly deleted.
_Avoid_: Deleted troupe, closed troupe

**Custodian**:
The site administrator, above all troupes, who appoints lead storytellers, manages the system itself, and can do anything in any troupe.
_Avoid_: Admin, superuser, site owner

**Staff**:
The people who run a troupe: its lead storyteller, storytellers, assistant storytellers and narrators.
_Avoid_: Admins, ST team

**Lead Storyteller**:
The troupe's one owner: a storyteller who can also rename the troupe, edit its description and delete it. Every troupe has exactly one.
_Avoid_: Head ST, LST (in names), co-lead

**Storyteller**:
A staff member who runs a troupe; storytellers and the lead storyteller are the only roles that manage the troupe's staff and house rules, and edit its profile.
_Avoid_: ST (in names), GM, admin

**Assistant Storyteller**:
A staff member who can do everything a storyteller can except manage staff, house rules and connectors, and edit the troupe's profile.
_Avoid_: AST (in names), sub-ST

**Narrator**:
A staff member who can do whatever a player in the troupe can, and also answer downtime actions assigned to them or to any narrator. A narrator never sees character sheets, not even for the actions they answer.
_Avoid_: Helper, volunteer

### Characters

**Character**:
A persona a player plays, belonging to exactly one splat. It belongs to its player and lives in their vault. It plays in one home troupe, which accepts it and holds its sheet, and can visit other troupes. A character is never deleted.
_Avoid_: Sheet (that is the character's record, not the character), PC

**PC**:
A player character: built by its player through character creation, and accepted by the troupe it asks to make its home.
_Avoid_: Primary, player character (in names)

**NPC**:
A character a troupe's storytellers or assistant storytellers make and keep for that troupe. It belongs to the troupe, needs no approval, takes no part in downtimes, and can be built quickly by setting ratings directly.
_Avoid_: Secondary, antecedence

**Scene**:
Several characters of one troupe put on one page of the Play View, side by side, by staff running a scene. Each card has the same taps, Undo and log as the character's own Play View. A troupe has as many named scenes as its staff make, kept on the server: every storyteller and assistant storyteller sees them all and can pin in any, and a scene's maker or the lead storyteller renames or removes it. Removing a scene leaves its characters as they are.
_Avoid_: Encounter, group view

**Title**:
A position a character holds in one troupe, such as Prince or Primogen, given by that troupe's storytellers or assistant storytellers. A title means nothing outside its troupe.
_Avoid_: Rank, office, court position

**Vault**:
A player's own characters, whether or not they are in any troupe.
_Avoid_: Library, roster

**Accepted Character**:
A character a troupe has accepted as its home troupe. Until then the character has no home troupe: its player edits it freely and it takes part in no downtimes.
_Avoid_: Approved character, draft (there are no per-troupe drafts)

**Visiting Troupe**:
A troupe a character is in that is not its home troupe. Its staff can see the sheet, but they approve nothing on it and award it no experience.
_Avoid_: Secondary troupe, guest troupe

**Diablerie**:
A vampire draining another of better Generation, which raises the diablerist's Generation by one. The home troupe's lead storyteller, storytellers and assistant storytellers record it on the sheet with its date (or approve the player's request to), and the sheet changes with it: Generation up one for free, techniques the new Generation cannot hold lost with their XP refunded, Humanity down one (or Beast traits at the next game for a character on a Path). Earlier buys keep their cost.
_Avoid_: Amaranth, level-up

**Embrace**:
A ghoul PC made a vampire in play by its sire. The home troupe's lead storyteller, storytellers and assistant storytellers run it from the ghoul's sheet, and its player chooses: it dies in the attempt, and is retired, or it becomes a vampire of its sire's clan, one generation weaker, with new clan and rarity merits, new starting disciplines, and what it bought in play repriced. What it cannot pay is XP debt.
_Avoid_: Conversion, turning, siring (in names)

**Retired Character**:
A character its player has stopped playing: hidden from everyday lists and out of all downtimes, but kept with its full history.
_Avoid_: Deleted, dead, inactive

**Splat**:
The kind of supernatural a character is, such as Vampire, Werewolf or Changeling.
_Avoid_: Venue, creature type, game line

**Domitor**:
The vampire a ghoul is bound to by its blood. A ghoul has no clan of its own: it takes its domitor's clan and bloodline, and its disciplines are its domitor's in-clan ones. The domitor is a PC or NPC in Horatio, or one known only by name.
_Avoid_: Master, regnant (that is any blood bond's), sire

**Vitae**:
The vampiric blood in a ghoul's Blood pool: at most 5 of its 10, the only part it can spend, and refilled only by drinking from a vampire. The rest of the pool is **human Blood**, which cannot be spent.
_Avoid_: Ghoul blood, vampire points

**Trait**:
A single rated or named entry on a character's sheet, such as a skill, merit, flaw, discipline or influence.
_Avoid_: Stat, ability

### Rules

**Rule Set**:
The generic rules for a splat that the system ships with, including what each trait costs.
_Avoid_: Core rules, ruleset (one word)

**Trait Description**:
The flavour text shown for a trait when a player is choosing it.
_Avoid_: Blurb, help text

**Rating**:
How many dots a character has in a trait.
_Avoid_: Level, value, score

**Free Rating**:
The part of a rating the character did not pay XP for, from creation or granted by another trait.
_Avoid_: Free value, bonus dots

**Cost Rule**:
A rule saying what XP a trait, or a whole category of traits, costs, possibly only under a condition such as being in-clan.
_Avoid_: Price, cost table

**Creation Rule**:
A rule saying what a new character gets free in a category: a number of picks at a given rating, or a budget of points.
_Avoid_: Creation pool, seed

**Creation Pick**:
Placing one pick from a creation rule on a trait, which gives the trait that many free dots.
_Avoid_: Starting dot assignment, freebie

**Starting Trait**:
A trait every new character of a splat starts with, free, such as Willpower.
_Avoid_: Default trait, seed trait

**Character Creation**:
The stage a new character is in until its player finishes it; creation picks can only be placed during it.
_Avoid_: Wizard, setup

**House Rule**:
A troupe's change to its rule set: a different cost, allowance or creation rule, or an action kind of its own. A house rule for a merit or flaw always names that one merit or flaw, and may carry its own text.
_Avoid_: Override, custom rule, homebrew

**Check**:
A rule about what a sheet may hold rather than what it costs, such as a clan-only merit or two flaws that cannot be taken together. A change that breaks one is refused, with the reason and the page.
_Avoid_: Validation, restriction, prerequisite (one kind of check)

**House Merit**:
A merit or flaw a troupe makes itself, marked ★ as that troupe's wherever it shows. Only the troupe's own characters hold one: those whose home troupe it is, those being built for it, and its NPCs; never one only visiting. Each is either given by staff only, or available when building for the troupe.
_Avoid_: Custom merit, homebrew merit

**Troupe Rules**:
A rule set with one troupe's house rules applied. A character plays by its home troupe's troupe rules, or by the plain rule set if it has no home troupe.
_Avoid_: Effective rules, local rules

### Character records

**Accepted Sheet**:
The character's sheet as its home troupe accepted it. The player cannot lower a rating below it; only storytellers take accepted dots away.
_Avoid_: Approved sheet, current sheet

**Reconciliation**:
Finding and settling, one by one, the differences between a character's purchases and starting dots and the troupe rules of the home troupe it is joining. A character whose differences are not all settled is not let into the troupe.
_Avoid_: Conversion, re-costing, audit

**Settlement**:
The XP change that settles one reconciliation difference, recorded alongside the purchase it corrects.
_Avoid_: Adjustment, correction

**Purchase**:
A trait bought for a character, recorded with the cost paid at the time; only reconciliation can change it, never a later rule change.
_Avoid_: Spend, buy

**Cart**:
Changes to a sheet a player has gathered on the Sheet tab but not yet made: raises, lowers, new traits, swaps and creation picks. Spend XP makes them all at once, or none if any is refused. It is kept in the player's browser, one per character.
_Avoid_: Basket, pending changes, draft

**XP Entry**:
One line in a character's XP history: an award of XP, or a purchase.
_Avoid_: Experience notation, XP log row

**XP Floor**:
The least XP a character in a troupe should have been given by now: the troupe's starting XP and every game's XP. A character built for the troupe starts at it, and one joining below it is offered a top-up.
_Avoid_: Minimum XP, baseline

**XP Ceiling**:
The most XP a character in a troupe could have been given by now: the floor and every downtime's XP for completing it. A character joining above it is shown to staff, who keep or take away what is above.
_Avoid_: Maximum XP, XP cap

**Self-entered XP**:
XP a player adds to their own character while it has no home troupe, such as for one played elsewhere. The troupe it makes its home accepts or removes it.
_Avoid_: Self-award, bonus XP

**XP Debt**:
XP a character owes, from its Embrace or put on it by its home troupe's staff, who can also forgive it: its XP history below nothing. All the XP it earns goes to the debt until it is paid off, and nothing that costs XP is bought until then.
_Avoid_: Negative XP, XP loan

**Long Text**:
A piece of free-form writing attached to a character, such as its background or history.
_Avoid_: Notes, bio, extended text

**Shared Background**:
A Haven, Herd, Allies or Contacts that characters in a coterie, pack or gang pool together, each putting in dots of their own; the group's rating is what is put in, never more than 5. A troupe's storytellers and assistant storytellers set one up and keep its log; the players of the characters that have put dots in see it, and log uses for their own. It is a record only: it changes no rating on any sheet.
_Avoid_: Pool, group background, coterie background (in names)

**Shared Background Entry**:
One line in a shared background's log: a character putting dots in or taking them out, or drawing on it, with when, what for, an optional note and who logged it.
_Avoid_: Contribution row, use record

**Portrait**:
The picture shown for a character or troupe.
_Avoid_: Avatar, image, photo

**Printable Sheet**:
The layout of a character's sheet as it stands, for printing and bringing to a game. Discipline dots awaiting approval are marked.
_Avoid_: PDF, printout, character card

**Sheet Version**:
One sheet frozen when a troupe accepted the character as its home: the accepted sheet.
_Avoid_: Snapshot, revision

**Approval**:
The home troupe's storytellers' or assistant storytellers' decision on a home troupe change or a discipline purchase. Nothing else on a sheet is approved: edits take effect at once.
_Avoid_: Sign-off, review

**Notification**:
Something in a person's bell. A task is work waiting on them, such as an approval to decide; it goes when the work is done. A message tells them something happened, such as downtime results arriving; it stays until read.
_Avoid_: Alert, inbox item

**Discipline Purchase**:
A discipline dot bought once the character has a home troupe. The experience is spent at once and the dot shows as awaiting approval until the home troupe decides; declining, withdrawing or leaving the troupe takes the dot back and refunds the experience.
_Avoid_: Discipline request, teaching

**Home Troupe**:
The one troupe that holds a character's sheet: it accepts the character, awards its experience, approves its discipline purchases, and runs the downtime cycles it takes part in.
_Avoid_: Main troupe, primary troupe

**Home Troupe Change**:
A player's request to give a character a home troupe or move it to a new one. The player proposes how each reconciliation difference is settled, and the new troupe's storytellers or assistant storytellers confirm or change each one when they decide; it takes effect from that troupe's next cycle.
_Avoid_: Transfer

### Downtimes

**Downtime**:
What a character does between games, submitted by the player and answered by staff.
_Avoid_: DT (in names), between-game actions

**Downtime Cycle**:
One round of downtimes for a troupe, with an open date, a close date and a grace period.
_Avoid_: Month, period, sheet

**Downtime Form**:
The questions a downtime cycle asks, started as a copy of the troupe's previous form.
_Avoid_: Template, Google Form

**Question**:
One prompt on a downtime form; either an action question or a custom question.
_Avoid_: Field, item

**Action Question**:
A question that spends one of the character's actions of a given action kind.
_Avoid_: Action field

**Custom Question**:
A question storytellers or assistant storytellers add that spends no action, such as a nomination or plot prompt.
_Avoid_: Extra question, survey question

**Action Kind**:
A category of downtime action, such as general, feeding, investigation, or elite or underworld influence.
_Avoid_: Action type, category

**Influence Action**:
One named thing a character does with Elite or Underworld influence in a month, such as "5: Lockdown"; each type has its own list. A general action has a level, and needs a rating at least that, or one less with one of the character's own specialisations of the type. A conflict action (Attack, Block, Boost, Defend, or a discovery block) is aimed at other influence and has no level.
_Avoid_: Influence level, influence use

**Influence Log**:
A troupe's record of every influence action taken in it: logged from downtime answers, spent at games, entered by staff or imported. Its storytellers and assistant storytellers keep it; players never see it, only their own characters' entries. Entries belong to the troupe, so they stay when a character leaves it.
_Avoid_: Influence sheet, Elite Infl. / UW Infl. (the old spreadsheet's tabs)

**Influence Entry**:
One line in the influence log: when the action was taken, to the minute, who took it (a character, or a name for someone not in Horatio), its type, specialisation, action and purpose, how many times it was taken, its discovery blocks, and where it came from. Each has a short id such as E12 or UW7.
_Avoid_: Influence row, influence record

**Influence History**:
A character's own influence entries, from the log of every troupe it has acted in, newest first. Its player and its home troupe's storytellers and assistant storytellers see it; it never shows another character's entries, nor blocks others placed.
_Avoid_: Influence log (that is the troupe's), influence sheet

**Influence Lookup**:
Gossip and Insider Trading (Elite) or Word on the Street (Underworld): an influence action answered from the influence log. It finds others' recent actions of its type in any of the character's specialisations of it, never who took them. Its strength is how many actions the character spends on it in one cycle, and it looks back the troupe's lookback in days from the cycle's close.
_Avoid_: Search, investigation

**Discovery Block**:
An influence action that hides one influence entry from lookups. Each accepted block adds one to the entry's blocks, and a lookup needs more strength than that to find it. It can be paid with either type's actions, and only general actions can be blocked.
_Avoid_: Cover-up, hide

**Participant**:
A character taking part in a downtime cycle. Participants are the characters whose home troupe runs the cycle when it opens; storytellers and assistant storytellers can add or remove participants afterwards, but only characters whose home troupe it is.
_Avoid_: Roster entry, entrant

**Action Allowance**:
How many actions of each action kind a character may take in one downtime cycle, fixed when the cycle opens; unused actions are lost.
_Avoid_: Action count, slots

**Allowance Rule**:
A rule saying how a trait adds to or removes from an action allowance.
_Avoid_: Bonus, modifier

**Allowance Adjustment**:
A storyteller's or assistant storyteller's change to one character's action allowance for one cycle, with a stated reason.
_Avoid_: Override

**Submission**:
A character's answers to one downtime cycle's form.
_Avoid_: Entry, form response

**Response**:
A staff member's answer to one question in a submission.
_Avoid_: Result, reply, outcome

**Assignment**:
Who is to write a response: one staff member, or any narrator. Only storytellers and assistant storytellers assign.
_Avoid_: Colour, task owner, Any Staff

**Any Narrator**:
An assignment that any of the troupe's narrators may accept, after which it becomes theirs.
_Avoid_: Shared pool, general tasks

**Decline**:
A narrator turning down an action assigned to them or accepted from any narrator, which sends it back to the storytellers and assistant storytellers to reassign.
_Avoid_: Reject, unassign, give back

**Delivery**:
Sending a submission's responses to its player.
_Avoid_: Send, publish

**Connector**:
A way out of Horatio that a troupe has set up and switched on, such as its Discord server or email. Each sends the whole result, or only an alert that it is ready, and has a test mode that sends everything to a test target instead. In-app delivery is always on and is not a connector.
_Avoid_: Integration, plugin, webhook

**Channel**:
The route one delivery took: `horatio` (in-app), `discord` or `email`. A troupe's connectors decide which channels a delivery goes out on.
_Avoid_: Medium, transport
